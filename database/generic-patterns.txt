! adblockker generic ad-path rules
!
! These block by URL *path*, on ANY domain, so an ad served from a brand-new
! host that no blocklist has yet is still caught the moment it uses a standard
! ad-tech endpoint. They are compiled into their own declarativeNetRequest
! ruleset (rules/generic.json).
!
! Every entry here is deliberately specific. Generic fragments like "/ad/" or
! "/ads/" are intentionally omitted: they match legitimate paths too often.
! Only high-confidence ad-tech path markers belong in this file.
